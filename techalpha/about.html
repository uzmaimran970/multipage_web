About TechAlpha

Our story, our mission and the people behind it.

My story

TechAlpha began with one idea: digital skills should be available to everyone, not only people in big cities or universities. Starting with a few small lessons online, we have grown into a learning space for students, teachers, families and homemakers.

(Apni asli kahani yahan likhein.)

Our mission

To give every person simple, affordable and practical digital skills, so they can learn, earn and grow.

Our vision

A community where anyone, anywhere, can build a career with technology.
